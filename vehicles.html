Lucky Winner · Plate I

Five vehicles, five economies

Drawn to one scale, one line weight, one baseline. The book does character through machinery: what a person drives, how they bought it, and whether anyone was meant to notice.

Jon

The Lexus GX

Bought used, based on the reliability data alone. Body-on-frame, a Land Cruiser underneath, almost nothing on it that can break. The grey man's ride.

serious capability, concealed, never once used

Jon

The Yota

1990 Toyota pickup, 3VZ-E V6, flat Tremclad grey. The box rusted off and he built a flatdeck. Cab corners cut out, new steel welded in. KC lights on the headache rack. Started weekly, going nowhere.

two years of weekends, then one, mostly alone

The dually

2019 Dodge, the big diesel, the rear axle wearing four tires instead of two. A hundred and ten thousand dollars of truck with seven years still to run on it.

announced him long before he did
